The quotes don't compare

Three say "included tax". Only one actually is. Two say "ex-works" and nobody mentions freight.

The adders are in prose

"Invoice costs extra." "Other colors are separate." "Price is negotiable for volume." Translation: your unit price is not your unit price.

The cheapest one is a trap

40% below the others usually means a skipped process, a substituted alloy, or a repricing the week you place the PO.

Median ¥42. The ¥25 quote is 60% of that — below the line, the report stops you from comparing it apples-to-apples and asks you to verify scope first.

Three questions to send back before you sign

Tax basisIs this price with 13% VAT? Can you issue a special VAT invoice?
Scope edgesWhat about tooling, freight, and samples — included or extra?
Price validityHow long is this price good for? Can you lock it for 30 days?

Every line traces back to a sentence in the original. If the supplier didn't write it, the report says "not stated" — it never fills in a number for them.

Questions

Is this "AI that quotes for me"?

No. We don't quote, we check. Quoting is your supplier's job. Our job is to turn what they wrote into a basis you can compare — especially the parts they left out. An AI-invented price is worse than no price.

Do you work with Chinese-language quotes?

That's the whole point. Chinese quotes, bilingual quotes, and English quotes from Chinese trading companies are all fine — currency and freight basis get normalized and shown explicitly.

What happens to my drawings and quotes?

Used to produce that one report. Not used for training, not resold, not shown publicly. Supplier names are masked by default. Happy to sign an NDA.

Why not free?

Free things don't answer to you. Nine dollars is what makes me read your quote carefully instead of running it through a script.

Can you also source the part?

Not yet — that's the next step, and I'd rather ship a check you can trust first than a marketplace with nobody in it.
